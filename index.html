About the Summit

Championing Women. Celebrating Excellence.

The 11th Annual Women Power Summit & Awards is a highly process-driven recognition and a neutral platform to celebrate business excellence, with 30 award categories for women entrepreneurs and 10 award categories for ecosystem enablers — evaluated through a stringent, transparent jury process.

Our Mission

To understand and address challenges faced by women entrepreneurs and celebrate excellence across MSMEs, startups, and ecosystem enablers through a rigorous, fair recognition platform.

40 Award Categories

30 categories exclusively for women entrepreneurs / women-led startups + 10 open categories for startup ecosystem enablers — recognising impact across every sector of India's economy.

Organised by Billennium Divas

Founded in 2017, Billennium Divas Private Limited is India's premier community for women entrepreneurs and changemakers, dedicated to driving inclusive growth and equitable societal progress.

Getting There

How to Reach NSE, BKC

🚇
By Metro

BKC Station on Metro Line 2A & 7 — just a 5-minute walk to NSE. The most convenient option; avoids BKC traffic entirely. Highly recommended.

🚗
By Road / Cab

Via Western Express Highway or Eastern Freeway. Ample parking available at BKC. Book Uber/Ola to drop at NSE Exchange Plaza Gate.

✈️
From Airport

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport is ~8 km away. Allow 20–40 minutes by taxi depending on traffic. Pre-book for event day.

For Outstation Delegates

Nearby Accommodations

Trident BKC

Bandra Kurla Complex · Walking distance · Preferred hotel for corporate delegations. Book early as BKC hotels fill fast.

The St. Regis Mumbai

Lower Parel · ~15 minutes · 5-star property ideal for outstation attendees wanting premium comfort and proximity.

Hyatt Regency Mumbai

Andheri East · ~20 minutes · Close to the airport, great value for delegates arriving the evening before the summit.
